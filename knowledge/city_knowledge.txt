ROAD INFORMATION

Road A:
Road A is a major city road.
Road A should be avoided when blocked.
Road A is an important emergency transportation corridor.

Road B:
Road B is an alternative route from the City Center toward the Hospital.
Road B is generally suitable for emergency vehicles.
Road B should be preferred when Road A is blocked.

Road C:
Road C experiences congestion during high traffic conditions.
Road C should not be preferred for urgent emergency transportation
when traffic exceeds 70 percent.


WEATHER GUIDELINES

Heavy rain:
Heavy rain can reduce road safety and increase travel time.
Emergency vehicles should use safer alternate routes when possible.

Normal weather:
Normal weather generally has minimal impact on emergency transportation.


EMERGENCY GUIDELINES

Accident:
Accidents may require emergency medical and police response.
Blocked roads near an accident can significantly delay emergency services.

Emergency response:
Emergency incidents should receive higher priority than normal traffic management.


TRAFFIC GUIDELINES

Traffic below 40 percent:
Normal traffic conditions.

Traffic between 40 and 70 percent:
Moderate congestion.

Traffic above 70 percent:
High congestion. Emergency routes should prioritize roads with lower traffic.
